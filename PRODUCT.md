# Product

## Register

product

## Users

LaporAI is for Indonesian employees who need to understand and review an annual individual tax return, hackathon judges testing a complete WebMCP workflow, and public-service teams evaluating agent-ready government forms. The primary task is completing a fictional employee return while the citizen and agent share the same visible state.

## Product purpose

LaporAI demonstrates how a government website can expose structured, state-aware actions to a browser agent without giving the agent control over credentials, file selection, declarations, signing, payment, or submission. Success means a judge can complete the same synthetic filing manually and with WebMCP, see every agent change, and compare the results.

## Brand personality

Calm, civic, precise. The product should lower the stress of a dense official form without becoming casual, cute, or promotional.

## Anti-references

- Government impersonation, official seals, DJP logos, or a `go.id` visual identity.
- Generic SaaS dashboards with floating cards, oversized metrics, gradients, glass effects, or decorative charts.
- Batik and kebaya motifs used as busy backgrounds behind form content.
- Playful fintech styling that makes tax filing look like a game.
- Agent interfaces that hide changes in a separate chat transcript.

## Design principles

- Keep the citizen oriented. Always show the current filing state, next valid action, and source of prefilled data.
- Put agent work in the form. Proposals, confirmations, changes, and undo controls appear beside the affected record.
- Make safety visible. Fictional-data labels and human-only actions stay obvious throughout the journey.
- Prefer official structure with plain explanations. Preserve tax terminology, then explain it without pretending to give tax advice.
- Measure the claim. Manual and WebMCP modes start from the same fixture and report comparable results.

## Accessibility and inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support full keyboard navigation, visible focus, 44px touch targets, semantic forms and tables, descriptive errors, reduced motion, zoom to 200 percent, and color-independent status communication. Use English for the interface, preserve official Indonesian tax terms, and keep sentences short enough for first-time filers.
